EMAIL OTP SETUP
===============

Why OTP is not arriving:
This project needs a real sender mailbox to send the OTP. The ZIP cannot contain your private mailbox password, so SMTP must be configured once on your computer.

Recommended Gmail setup:
1. Open your Google Account Security settings.
2. Turn on 2-Step Verification.
3. Create an App Password for this project.
4. Start run.bat and login as Admin.
5. Open OTP Email Setup.
6. Enter your Gmail address and App Password, then Save Email Settings.
7. Enter a test recipient and click Send Test OTP.
8. Only after the test email arrives should students register.

Alternative:
Run setup_email.bat and enter the sender Gmail address + App Password.

NEVER share your Gmail password or App Password in chat or commit .env to GitHub.
