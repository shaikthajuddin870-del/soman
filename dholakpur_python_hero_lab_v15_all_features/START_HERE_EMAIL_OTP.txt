EMAIL OTP - START HERE

The ZIP cannot contain your personal Gmail credentials.
On the first run, run.bat opens a one-time Gmail OTP setup wizard.

1. Turn on 2-Step Verification on the sender Google account.
2. Create a Google App Password.
3. In the setup window, enter the sender Gmail and the 16-character App Password.
4. The wizard tests the SMTP login before the website starts.
5. Create a student account. The OTP is sent to the student's email.
6. The student account is created only after the OTP is verified.

Important:
- Do NOT use your normal Gmail password.
- Do NOT share the App Password in chat.
- Keep the local project folder private because .env contains the SMTP password.
